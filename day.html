The ten days

How this page works
  1. One card, one step. The Now card always shows the next step of the day. Start runs its timer; Done ticks it and moves to the next. A break or a meal starts by itself when the step before it ends on its timer.
  2. Steps with parts — a CCS case is 12:30 then 10:00 of feedback; a review is 30 · stand 10 · 30 · stand 10 · 30. The stand starts by itself; the next chunk and the feedback wait for your tap.
  3. The sound. A step's end is a rising four-note melody played twice, with a long vibration where the device allows it (Android; iPad and iPhone do not vibrate from a web app). One minute before the end: two soft notes. If you do not tick within a minute, the melody repeats — once a minute, five times at most (Settings).
  4. The other pages still work. Run a block in the Block page, a review in the Review page, a case in the CCS page: this card shows it running, and the step ticks itself when it is saved. The 20-minute break after a block, a review or a case is the same break on every page.
  5. Tick by hand when you did a step without a timer: tap its box. Tap a row to make it the current step. Tap a ticked box to untick — a step you untick stays unticked until you tick it; the page never re-ticks it on its own.
  6. Hidden app. Allow notifications (Settings → Background & sound): the end of a step, and the reminders, arrive as notifications when the page is hidden. On iPad, open the pacer from the Home Screen icon. Whatever was missed is caught up the moment you come back.
  7. The day rolls over at 4:00 AM, so a night that runs past midnight stays one day. Days are kept for the ten-day strip.

Keys: Space Start or Done · N next part · P pause · E +5:00 · D done.

Case numbers: the first two are case 1 and case 2 of that day, a third is the retry for the 3rd slot. An L after a number (94L) is a 20-minute case → 25:00 on the clock; without it the case gets 12:30. The Now card lets you switch 12:30 / 25:00 before Start either way. Sequences: study · tonight · rehearsal · between · interface · minimum · exam · off. A date that is not listed gets a study day.
