How this page works
  1. One card, one step. The Now card always shows the next step of the day. Start runs its timer; Done ticks it and moves to the next. A break or a meal starts by itself when the step before it ends on its timer.
  2. Your order. The blocks in a row with the same break between them, then the CCS cases — each with its feedback right after — then lunch, then the fast pass, then the reviews. On a break card, Lunch now · break later ⇄ trades the two in one tap; on the lunch card, Lunch later · break now ⇄ does the reverse (dinner the same way). Move ▲ / Move ▼ moves any step earlier or later for today. Settings → The study day sets the number of blocks and cases and where lunch usually sits.
  3. Every length can change, at any moment. Before Start: 12:30 / 25:00 on a case, 30 · 45 · 60 · 90 min on a review, −5:00 / +5:00 on the rest. While it runs: −5:00 · −1:00 · +1:00 · +5:00; 12:30 / 25:00 in the middle of a case (the time already used stays); Feedback ▸ or Stand ▸ ends a part early (two taps, like Skip). Settings can be saved while a timer runs.
  4. Steps with parts — a CCS case is its clock, then 10:00 of feedback; a review is 30-minute chunks with a 10-minute stand between them. The stand starts by itself; the next chunk and the feedback wait for your tap.
  5. The fast pass. One step for all the blocks of the morning, 10:00 each (8 · 10 · 12 before Start). It sorts, it does not study: a right answer → next; a lucky guess → the Educational Objective only; a wrong answer → the Educational Objective and the paragraph of the answer you picked. The big button is Block 2 ▸, one tap, when a block is done early; at 10:00 the next block starts by itself. What you mark there is what gets the 8 minutes in the reviews.
  6. Reviews, lowest score first. On the fast-pass card (before Start) or on the first review card, type today's scores once (B1 … B6) — or type each score on the Block page's summary. Every review step then names its block, lowest score first, and gets 5:00 per wrong question and 1:00 per right one (Settings → Review per block for a fixed length). The Review page lists the blocks in the same order.
  7. The totals are measured. A step that ran on a timer — here or in its own page — counts the time it really took. A step ticked by hand counts its planned length. A skipped step counts nothing.
  8. The sound. A step's end is a rising four-note melody played twice, with a long vibration where the device allows it (Android; iPad and iPhone do not vibrate from a web app). One minute before the end: two soft notes. If you do not tick within a minute, the melody repeats — once a minute, five times at most (Settings).
  9. The other pages still work. Run a block in Block, a review in Block review, a case in CCS case (its feedback opens in CCS review): this card shows it running, and the step ticks itself when it is saved. The 20-minute break after a block, a review or a case is the same break on every page.
  10. Tick by hand when you did a step without a timer: tap its box. Tap a row to make it the current step; during a break, that ends the break. Tap a ticked box to untick — a step you untick stays unticked until you tick it; the page never re-ticks it on its own.
  11. Hidden app. Allow notifications (Settings → Background & sound): the end of a step, and the reminders, arrive as notifications when the page is hidden. On iPad, open the pacer from the Home Screen icon. Whatever was missed is caught up the moment you come back.
  12. The day rolls over at 4:00 AM, so a night that runs past midnight stays one day. Days are kept for the calendar at the bottom.

Keys: Space Start or Done · N next part · P pause · E +5:00 · D done.
