    Rules for this review
    1. Two timers. Timer A: 30-min chunks, 3 chunks = 90 min per block, hard stop; stand 3–5 min between chunks. Timer B: 5 min per wrong question, +3 if you need it = 8 max. Rings → you move.
    2. Correct questions: no Timer B. Educational Objective, 30 seconds. A lucky guess counts as wrong.
    3. Don't re-read the stem. Educational Objective → correct-answer paragraph → the paragraph for the choice you picked. Skip the other wrong-choice paragraphs.
    4. Calculation question → draw the 2×2 and redo it yourself before reading their math.
    5. Write one line: "Rule: ___" in your own words. Ten words max. Tag the miss with one letter.
    6. Chunk 3 ends with questions left → Educational Objective only, 30 s each, tagged K. The app switches to that by itself.
    7. Not allowed: UWorld library, a new sheet, YouTube, a podcast, a "quick" lookup, Claude per question. Parking lot instead.
