    Rules for this review
    1. Two timers. Timer A: 30-min chunks, 3 chunks = 90 min per block, hard stop; stand 10 min between chunks (the break after a chunk). Timer B: 5 min per wrong question, +3 if you need it = 8 max. Rings → you move.
    2. Order: wrong questions first (UWorld Results → Show: Incorrect), then the right ones with whatever time is left. The app walks you through it in that order; "Any order" in the setup if you want to mix.
    3. Right questions: no Timer B. Educational Objective, 60 seconds each. A lucky guess counts as wrong.
    4. Don't re-read the stem. Educational Objective → correct-answer paragraph → the paragraph for the choice you picked. Skip the other wrong-choice paragraphs.
    5. Calculation question → draw the 2×2 and redo it yourself before reading their math.
    6. Write one line: "Rule: ___" in your own words. Ten words max. Tag the miss with one letter.
    7. Chunk 3 ends with questions left → Educational Objective only, 60 s each, tagged K. The app switches to that by itself (or ends the review, if you set that in Settings).
    8. When the review ends, the 20-minute break starts by itself and shows on every pacer page until the next block, review or case starts.
    9. Pressed the wrong button? "Change choice" sends the running question back to the chooser; the clock on that question keeps counting. "Undo last question" reopens the one you just closed.
    10. Not allowed: UWorld library, a new sheet, YouTube, a podcast, a "quick" lookup, Claude per question. Parking lot instead.

    Hidden app: iPad — open the pacer from the Home Screen icon and allow notifications, otherwise keep it in split screen beside UWorld (a hidden web app on iPad cannot play sound). Android — notifications plus "Keep running in the background" in Settings. Mac — notifications. A chunk that ended while the app was hidden is caught up when you come back: Timer A stops at the chunk end, not when you return.
