      Rules for this block

      Write this line on top of your scratch paper before you press Start:

      1. At each checkpoint the clock must read at least the target. Behind (clock lower than the number) → the next 5 are flag-at-50 questions: last line, stem once, best answer, flag, move.
      2. Ahead → the extra goes to flagged questions after Q20.
      3. First pass = easy ones. At 50 s with no big picture: flag, best guess, move.
      4. Mixed blocks and the real exam: skip biostats and drug ads on pass 1.
      5. 2:00 left → fill every blank with a guess. Never leave a blank.
      6. The block ends by itself at 0:00, or when you tap the last question (with flags: when the last flag is resolved). Tapped by mistake? Undo, or "back into the block" on the summary.

      The time on this question is shown in minutes: amber at 1:00, orange at 1:30, red at 1:52 (the 37:30 ÷ 20 budget) with its own soft sound once per question; violet "flag & move" at 0:50 after a behind checkpoint.
