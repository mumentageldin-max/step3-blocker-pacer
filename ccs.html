    Rules for a CCS case
    1. Real time is the only clock that can end the case. Simulated time moves only when you advance it, and no order is carried out until it does. Advance early, in small steps ("with next available result", "call me as needed").
    2. Read once: intro, vitals, history. One line: who, how sick, where. Unstable → the ED protocol. Stable → the office protocol.
    3. Unstable: IV access, O2, cardiac monitor, pulse ox, then a focused exam, then treat. Stable: complete exam, then labs and first treatment.
    4. Orders as a list, confirm the exact item. Primum has many look-alike names; pick the specific one. Doses are automatic; you choose the route.
    5. After every batch of results: treat → interval history + repeat exam → move the patient (ward / ICU / home) → consult if indicated. Then advance again.
    6. Counseling, vaccines and the follow-up appointment go in as soon as the plan is settled, not at the warning. Stop what's finished (fluids, monitor, IV).
    7. The software warns you at about two minutes of real time. From then on: no exam, no location change, no clock. Only final orders. Then Exit Case.
    8. Finishing early is fine. Unused case time becomes break time on exam day. Don't use the clock up for its own sake.

    Exam day 4: 13–14 cases, about half of them long ones. ~4½ hours of case time, 85 minutes of breaks: 10 after case 3, lunch 25 after case 7, 10 after case 10, 10 after case 12, 30 spare.

    After the case: the feedback review timer is 10:00 for a 12:30 case and 15:00 for a 25:00 case (Settings). Read the feedback once, write the one order you missed, score, save. A retry is a different day.
