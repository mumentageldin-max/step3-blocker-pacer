      The time stamp is when you tapped it. Stats uses the first clock advance and the counseling time. Undo reverts the last tap; "Unstable / Stable" can be switched any time.

      Rules for a CCS case
      1. Real time is the only clock that can end the case. Simulated time moves only when you advance it, and no order is carried out until it does. In the ED advance "with next available result", in small steps. "Call / see me as needed" sends the patient home and effectively ends the case — only when you are done.
      2. Read once: intro, vitals, history. One line: who, how sick, where. Unstable → HAVOC + 5-box exam. Stable → full exam. Pulse ox is not in the vitals. Home meds show as Day 1 @00:00: keep or cancel.
      3. Order entry (USMLE): the clerk recognizes the first three characters and shows a list — type 3–4 letters, read, pick. No verbs, no doses. Drugs by trade or generic name, never a class. You pick the route (IV, PO, IM, SQ, IN…). No PRN; IV fluids are continuous. Not recognized? Retype it another way.
      4. After every batch of results: treat → interval history + repeat exam → move the patient (ward / ICU / home) → consult if it changes care. Then advance again. Admission = ADMIT CORT.
      5. SAVE before the warning: Stop finished orders, Appointment, Vaccines/screening, Educate. After the Case-end screen you can no longer examine, change location, schedule a follow-up or advance the clock — only write and cancel orders.
      6. Scoring (USMLE): indicated actions earn, risky non-indicated actions subtract, timing and sequence count ("after a delay in simulated time may receive no credit"), restraint is scored when it is the standard. Counseling carries little weight unless relevant, but is not penalized.
      7. The software warns you at about two minutes of real time. From then on: final orders only. Then Exit Case — the case does not end until you click it or real time runs out.
      8. Finishing early is fine. Unused case time becomes break time on exam day (your letter: 85 min + unused time). Don't use the clock up for its own sake.

      Exam day 4: 13–14 cases, about half of them long ones. ~4½ hours of case time, 85 minutes of breaks: 10 after case 3, lunch 25 after case 7, 10 after case 10, 10 after case 12, 30 spare.

      After the case: the feedback review timer is 10:00 for a 12:30 case and 15:00 for a 25:00 case (Settings). Read the feedback once, write the one order you missed, score, save. A retry is a different day.
